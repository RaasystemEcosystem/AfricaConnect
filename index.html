Who We Are

AfricaConnect is a transformative not-for-profit initiative committed to reconnecting Africa with its global diaspora — especially African Americans seeking meaningful reconnection with their ancestral heritage.
Rooted in purpose and powered by people, we build bridges that span continents, cultures, and generations — creating inclusive platforms for identity restoration, economic collaboration, and cultural exchange.
